## Rules I follow

1. **Ask permission first.** Explain the purpose: a university-style portfolio project, nothing sold, no medical details needed.
2. **No real names or medical details** in anything I publish. Interviewees are P1, P2, R1 and so on.
3. **Ask about real past events, not opinions.** "Tell me about the last time you…" gets facts; "Would you like an app?" gets polite yeses.
4. **Don't pitch the app** until the last question. Showing the idea early biases every answer.
5. **Write down numbers and exact quotes.** "Waited about 2 hours" beats "waited long".
6. **Listen more than talk.** Follow up with "Why?", "What happened next?", "Can you give an example?"

## After all interviews

Fill the synthesis table in each notes file, then look across all of them for:
- the **top 3 pain points**, with how many people mentioned each
- anything that **surprised me** or changes the MVP (this is checkpoint question 3)
- **requirement ideas** to carry into the BRD and PRD
