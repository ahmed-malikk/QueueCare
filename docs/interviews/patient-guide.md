# Interview guide: patients

**Who:** 2–3 people who have waited at a clinic, family doctor or hospital OPD in the last few months. Family, friends or neighbours are fine. **Time:** about 10 minutes each.
**Goal:** understand the waiting experience from the patient's side, especially not knowing how long the wait will be.

Each question has a simple Roman Urdu version in *italics* for anyone more comfortable in Urdu.

## Opening (say this)

> "I'm doing a portfolio project about clinic waiting times. Can I ask you about the last time you went to a clinic? I don't need any medical details or your name, just how the waiting went."
> *"Main clinic mein intezaar ke baare mein ek project bana raha hoon. Kya main aap se aapke pichle clinic visit ke baare mein kuch pooch sakta hoon? Bimari ya naam ki zaroorat nahi, sirf intezaar kaisa raha."*

Note: age range, who they went for (themselves, a child, a parent), type of clinic.

## 1. The last visit (a real story)

1. Tell me about the last time you went to a clinic. What happened from when you arrived?
   *Aakhri dafa jab aap clinic gaye, pohanchne ke baad kya kya hua?*
2. How did you get your turn: a token, a number, your name in a register, or something else?
   *Aapko baari kaise mili: token, number, register mein naam, ya kuch aur?*
3. How long did you wait before seeing the doctor? Was that normal?
   *Doctor se milne se pehle kitni der intezaar kiya? Kya yeh aam baat hai?*
4. Did anyone tell you how long it would take? Was it accurate?
   *Kya kisi ne bataya ke kitni der lagegi? Kya woh sahi nikla?*

## 2. While waiting

5. What did you do while you waited? Could you leave and come back?
   *Intezaar ke dauraan aap ne kya kiya? Kya aap bahar ja kar wapis aa sakte the?*
6. How did you know when it was your turn? Did you ever worry you'd miss it?
   *Aapko kaise pata chala ke aapki baari aa gayi? Kya darr tha ke baari nikal na jaye?*
7. Did anyone go ahead of you? How did you feel about it? Did you know why?
   *Kya koi aap se pehle chala gaya? Aapko kaisa laga? Kya pata tha kyun?*

## 3. What matters

8. What was the worst part of the wait?
   *Intezaar ka sab se bura hissa kya tha?*
9. If someone is more urgent, for example a child with high fever, is it fair for them to go first? What would make it feel fair to you?
   *Agar koi zyada emergency mein ho, jaise tez bukhar wala bacha, to kya usay pehle bhejna theek hai? Aapko kya cheez fair lagegi?*

## 4. Only now: the idea

10. If you could see your token number and how long you'd wait on your phone, would you use it? What would you do with that time?
    *Agar aap phone par apna token number aur intezaar ka andaaza dekh saken, to kya istemaal karenge? Us waqt mein kya karenge?*
11. Would you rather get it on WhatsApp, a website, or see a screen in the waiting room?
    *Aap WhatsApp, website, ya waiting room ki screen, kis par dekhna pasand karenge?*
12. Is there anything else about waiting at clinics that bothers you?
    *Clinic par intezaar ke baare mein koi aur baat jo aapko pareshan karti hai?*

## Closing

> "Thank you, that's really helpful."
> *"Bohat shukriya, is se bohat madad mili."*
